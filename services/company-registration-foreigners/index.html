SCOPE

What Pinpoint helps prepare

1. Structure and document readiness

  • Company name options and business-objective summary

  • Registered office documents and coordinator checklist
  • Capital planning notes for DBD, work permit, FBA/FBL, or BOI follow-up

2. Foreign-business screening workflow

  • Thai-majority versus foreign-company flag

OFFICIAL CONTEXT

Important 2026 checkpoints

DBD fees depend on registered capital

BOI guidance lists MOA and company registration fees by registered capital, with minimum and maximum fee ranges. Pinpoint checks the fee logic against the planned capital before filing.

FBL timing is separate from incorporation

Foreign Business License consideration can take up to 60 days after the accepted application reaches the relevant review stage, with license issuance after approval. Pre-acceptance document quality matters.

Tax and employer setup starts quickly

Tax ID, VAT threshold monitoring, and social security/employer registration should be placed on the first-month checklist when they apply to the business.
