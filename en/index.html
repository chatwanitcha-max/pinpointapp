FAQ

Common questions before choosing a Bangkok accounting firm

These answers help owners compare scope, documents, timing, and communication before starting accounting, tax, DBD, visa, work permit, or business-license work.

What services does Pinpoint Accounting & Service provide?

Pinpoint supports monthly accounting, VAT, withholding tax, year-end closing, company registration, DBD amendments, visa and work permit coordination, business licenses, payroll, social security, tax planning, audit preparation, company dissolution, and business-document preparation.

Does the team support foreign-owned companies?

Yes. The team can communicate in Thai and English and is familiar with cases involving foreign directors, foreign staff, company registration, work permit renewal, visa extension, and documents that need to be understood by both Thai and international managers.

What documents should be prepared before monthly accounting starts?

Useful starting documents include sales records, purchase records, tax invoices, bank statements, expense documents, prior VAT or withholding tax filings, company affidavit, DBD documents, and any existing accounting data from the previous provider.

Which areas does Pinpoint serve?

Pinpoint is based in Huai Khwang, Ratchada, Bangkok and serves Bangkok areas such as Rama 9, Sutthisan, Ladprao, Din Daeng, Asoke, and Sukhumvit. The team also supports clients across Thailand when documents can be handled online.
