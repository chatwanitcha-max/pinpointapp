Daily plan | 1-7 Oct 2026

click image buttons for web/map
WebMap
Thu, 1 Oct

Fly into Chiang Mai • Check in at Mae Rim • Relax with mountain views

Keep the first day light. After picking up the car and checking in, relax at Bantunglom Resort and enjoy a slow evening around Mae Rim.

WebMap
Fri, 2 Oct

Queen Sirikit Botanic Garden + Mae Rim scenic route

A low-stress nature day with garden views, mountain scenery, and an easy rhythm close to your Chiang Mai base.

WebMap
Sat, 3 Oct

Mae Kampong + San Kamphaeng Hot Springs

The signature slow-travel day. Explore Mae Kampong first, then continue to the natural hot springs in the afternoon.

WebMap
Sun, 4 Oct

Jing Jai Market + Chiang Mai Old Town + Doi Suthep

Start at Jing Jai Market, move through the old city and Tha Phae Gate, and end the day with the atmosphere and view at Doi Suthep.

WebMap
Mon, 5 Oct

Travel on to Nan • Check in at Phu Ka Na Nan Resort

Allow extra time for the transfer day, then keep the evening easy and rest in the natural setting of the resort.

WebMap
Tue, 6 Oct

Floating Road / Route 1256 + Doi Phu Kha + Bo Kluea + Sapan

The main nature day in Nan. Leave early for mountain scenery, scenic curves, and the calm village atmosphere around Sapan.

WebMap
Wed, 7 Oct

Nan Old Town + Wat Phumin + travel to Bangkok

Finish the northern leg with Nan Old Town and Wat Phumin before heading on to Bangkok for the 8-10 Oct stay.

Planning note: Chiang Mai works especially well with a rental car because the main highlights are spread around the outskirts and hills. If you want less road time on the way to Nan, we can later switch that transfer to a flight-based route.
