The most practical budget option. Current booking pages show public minivans from Chiang Mai to Pai from about THB 240-250, with departures starting around 6:30 AM and running roughly hourly. Most services use Arcade Bus Terminal 2.

Scenic but demanding
EN
Best if you want flexibility and viewpoint stops. The route is about 130-140 km via Route 1095 and usually takes 3-4 hours without long stops. Motorbike is only for confident riders because of the curves; for most travelers a rental car is safer.
Current car-rental pages in Chiang Mai advertise rates from about THB 990/day; motorbike options are also listed by AYA.

EN
Best for door-to-door comfort, luggage, or small groups. Kiwitaxi currently shows fixed-fare private cars from Chiang Mai Airport to Pai from about US$105 (around THB 3,800), while Klook also lists private transfer products for this route.
Road time is still about 3-4 hours, but you avoid transfers and terminal handling.
